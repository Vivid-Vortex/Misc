# 🗓️ Weekly Snack Ready Chart (for Meetings)

### 🔹 **Daily Basics (Always Ready)**

- **Roasted chana / peanuts** → keep a jar on your desk.
    
- **Jaggery cubes / small dates / raisins** → for quick glucose.
    
- **Almonds/Walnuts** → 5–6 pieces per day, soaked overnight if you prefer.
    
- **Fresh fruit basket** → bananas, apples, guavas, seasonal fruits.
    

---

### 🔹 **Weekly Prep Plan**

|Day|Prep Item|How to Prepare|Storage|Meeting Snack Combo|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|**Sunday Night**|Soak **moong / chana** for sprouting|Soak overnight, then drain and cover with a damp cloth|Store sprouts in fridge (lasts 3–4 days)|Mix sprouts + lemon + pinch of salt|
|**Monday Night**|Soak **almonds**|In a small bowl of water|Use next morning, lasts 2 days in fridge|6 soaked almonds + 1 banana|
|**Tuesday Night**|Make **yogurt mix jars**|Small bowl of curd/Greek yogurt + 1 spoon raisins/dates|Store covered, 2 days max|Yogurt bowl before meeting|
|**Wednesday Night**|Prepare **smoothie packs**|Pre-cut banana, oats, nuts in a zip bag|Freeze (use within 1 week)|Blend with milk in 2 mins|
|**Thursday Night**|Refill **nut jars**|Almonds, walnuts, pistachios|Airtight jar|Grab handful + 1 fruit|
|**Friday Night**|Roast **extra chana/peanuts**|Dry roast in kadhai, cool down|Airtight jar (lasts 2 weeks)|Roasted chana + jaggery|
|**Saturday Night**|Treat prep: **dark chocolate pieces**|Break bar into small 10–15g pieces|Airtight box|Chocolate + 5 nuts|

---

### 🔹 **Rotation Example (so you don’t get bored)**

- **Monday** → Almonds + Banana
    
- **Tuesday** → Roasted Chana + Jaggery
    
- **Wednesday** → Sprouted Moong Salad
    
- **Thursday** → Yogurt + Dates
    
- **Friday** → Peanut Butter + Brown Bread Slice
    
- **Saturday** → Dark Chocolate + Nuts
    
- **Sunday** → Fruit Smoothie (Banana + Oats + Milk)
    

---

### 🔑 Extra Tips

- Keep **a water bottle** near you → hydration = sharper brain.
    
- If meetings are **back-to-back**, prefer **lighter options** (sprouts, yogurt, fruit) over heavy ones (peanut butter sandwich).
    
- Keep snacks in **small jars/portions** → prevents overeating and sluggishness.
