---

## ✅ Answer: Smart Pre-Meeting Snack Options

The goal here is:

- **Steady energy** (not a sugar crash)
    
- **Brain fuel** (healthy fats + protein)
    
- **Easy digestion** (so you don’t feel heavy)
    
- **Quick availability**
    

Here are some practical combinations:

---

### 1. **Nut + Fruit Combo (Balanced Brain Fuel)**

- A small handful of **almonds/walnuts** (healthy fats, omega-3, protein)
    
- - 1 **banana or apple** (quick-release carbs for instant energy)
        

👉 This is the **simplest, quickest combo**. Nuts give sustained focus, fruit gives quick glucose for your brain.

---

### 2. **Roasted Chana (Chickpeas) + Jaggery**

- 1 small bowl of **roasted chana** (fiber + protein = stable energy)
    
- A small piece of **jaggery (gur)** for quick glucose
    

👉 This is a **classic Indian snack**, very effective for keeping alert without feeling heavy.

---

### 3. **Soaked Almonds or Sprouted Moong/Chana**

- Soak **5-6 almonds** overnight → eat them in the morning or just before meeting.
    
- Or sprouted **moong/chana** with a little lemon + pinch of salt.
    

👉 Sprouts give you **complex carbs + protein + B vitamins** = excellent for brain activity.

---

### 4. **Greek Yogurt + Dry Fruits**

- ½ cup **Greek yogurt / hung curd**
    
- Mix in **raisins or dates (1-2 pieces)** + a few nuts.
    

👉 Protein from yogurt + quick sugars from dried fruits = keeps you full and sharp.

---

### 5. **Peanut Butter + Whole Wheat Bread / Rice Cake**

- 1 slice **whole wheat bread** (or rice cracker)
    
- Spread with **peanut butter** (healthy fat + protein).
    

👉 Great if you like something more filling. Keeps energy steady for 1–2 hrs.

---

### 6. **Dark Chocolate + Nuts**

- A **small piece (10–15g) of dark chocolate (70%+)**
    
- - 5-6 **pistachios/cashews**.
        

👉 Dark chocolate has **caffeine + theobromine** → boosts alertness and focus. Nuts balance it with steady energy.

---

### 7. **Fruit Smoothie (Light & Refreshing)**

- Blend **1 banana + handful of oats + milk (or curd)**
    
- Add **1 tsp peanut butter** if you want extra protein.
    

👉 Smooth digestion + quick but steady energy.

---

## 🔑 Tips for Pre-Meeting Snacks

- **Eat 20–30 minutes before** the call → enough time for your brain to use the glucose.
    
- **Avoid heavy/oily foods** (chips, fried snacks) → they make you sluggish.
    
- **Avoid only-sugar foods** (like candy, too many dates) → you’ll get a quick spike but then a crash mid-meeting.
    
- **Hydrate** → even mild dehydration reduces focus. A glass of water or coconut water works wonders.
    

---

## ⚡ My Top 3 Practical Picks for You (Easy in India)

1. **Roasted chana + jaggery + water** (cheap, quick, balanced)
    
2. **Almonds/walnuts + banana** (classic brain fuel)
    
3. **Sprouted moong with lemon + salt** (if you pre-plan a bit)
