# ✅ Snacks for Energy & Focus Before a 1-Hour Meeting

### Principles:

- You don’t want only **high calories** (which can cause sluggishness).
    
- You want **slow-releasing carbs + protein + healthy fats** = sustained energy + sharper focus.
    
- A bit of **hydration & micronutrients** (like B-vitamins, magnesium) also help with mental clarity.

## 🥗 Practical Combinations (Balanced Snacks)

1. **Walnut + Date Combo** 🧠
    
    - 2–3 walnuts + 1 date → Omega-3 + quick sugar = sharp focus.
        
2. **Almonds + Raisins**
    
    - A classic trail-mix style snack, easy to keep handy.
        
3. **Peanut Butter + Fruit**
    
    - 1 apple/banana slice with peanut butter → brain fuel + satiety.
        
4. **Roasted Chickpeas + Sweet Potato**
    
    - Small bowl of roasted chana + ½ boiled sweet potato → balanced protein & slow carbs.
        
5. **Sprouted Moong Salad**
    
    - With lemon, onion, cucumber → refreshing, doesn’t make you drowsy.
        
6. **Cashews + Dried Figs**
    
    - Creamy cashew + fiber-rich fig = long-lasting energy.
        

---

## ⚡ My Recommendation (Before a 1-Hour Meeting)

- Best balance: **Small handful of mixed nuts (almonds + walnuts + pistachios) + 1 date/fig + ½ boiled sweet potato.**
    
- If you’re short on prep time: **Roasted chickpeas + raisins** (keeps you alert, not heavy).
    
- If you like refreshing options: **Sprouted moong with lemon**.
