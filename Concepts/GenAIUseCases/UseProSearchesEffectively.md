## 🔄 Chain Prompts — What & Why?

### ✔️ What it means:
Ask **follow-up questions** instead of starting over with new ones.

### ✔️ Why it helps:
- **Preserves context** so the model gives deeper insights
- **Saves requests** by covering multiple angles in one interaction
- **Enhances understanding** with layered answers (e.g., pros & cons, diagrams, optimizations)

### 💡 Example:
```plaintext
Q: Explain how HashMap works in Java.
↳ Follow-ups:
• Add pros & cons  
• Show internal classes used  
• Provide a diagram  
• Compare with ConcurrentHashMap  
````

---

## 📦 Batch-Style Prompting

### ✔️ What it means:

Group 3–5 related questions into **a single prompt** to save requests and get a cohesive answer.

### ✔️ Benefit:

* **One request instead of five**
* **Cohesive responses** that understand all questions in context

### 💬 Optimized Explanation:

> Use batch-style prompting: Group 5 related questions in one prompt to save requests and get a more connected response, instead of asking them separately.
