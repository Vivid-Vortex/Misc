## 🔑 **Strategies to Master Impromptu Speaking**

### 🧱 1. Use a Simple Structure (Always!)

Whenever you're put on the spot, use one of these basic formats to stay organized:

|Strategy|Explanation|Quick Example|
|---|---|---|
|**PREP**|Point → Reason → Example → Point|"Working from home is productive. Because it saves time. For example, I get 2 extra hours daily. So, yes, it improves output."|
|**Past-Present-Future**|Talk about history, current status, and what's next|"Mobile phones were once rare. Now they’re essential. In the future, they may replace wallets completely."|
|**Point-Counterpoint-Conclusion**|Share both sides, then your stance|"AI is useful. But it can be misused. Overall, we need cautious adoption."|
|**What-Why-How**|Explain a concept, justify it, then suggest implementation|"Cloud storage matters. Because data loss is costly. Use Google Drive, NAS, or backup scripts."|

---

### 🗣️ 2. Practice Thinking Aloud

Don't try to be perfect. Just **keep speaking** and **think as you go**. The idea is fluency, not perfection.

> ✨ "That’s an interesting topic. I haven’t thought about it much, but here’s what comes to mind..."

---

### ⏳ 3. Use a Timer (2–3 minutes)

Use a timer to simulate pressure. Keep your talk **under 3 minutes**. Focus on:

- Clear structure
    
- Transitions between ideas
    
- Speaking without filler words ("umm", "you know", etc.)
    

---

### 🎯 4. Practice with Random Topics

Make a list of 50 random topics (I’ll give you some below), pick one blindly, and speak on it using the structure you choose.

---

### 🧪 5. Record and Review

Record your 2-minute talks. Watch them after a few hours or the next day. Check:

- Voice modulation
    
- Eye contact (if on video)
    
- Filler words
    
- Clarity of thought

## 🎙️ Example Practice Prompt (With Strategy)

### Topic: _“Is technology making us less social?”_

Using **Point-Counterpoint-Conclusion**:

> "Technology has made communication faster and more accessible. Apps like WhatsApp or Teams let us connect instantly.  
> However, it often replaces face-to-face interactions, and we might lose depth in relationships.  
> In my view, while tech offers convenience, we must balance it with meaningful in-person conversations."

🕒 Practice Time: **1–2 minutes**
