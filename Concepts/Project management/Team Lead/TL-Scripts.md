Great question, Deepak!

The specific skill you’re developing here is a **combination of several interrelated soft skills**, but the core name for it in leadership and communication circles is:

---

### 🎯 **Facilitative Leadership**

or

### 🗣️ **Meeting Facilitation Skills**

---

It’s a blend of the following key sub-skills:

|Skill Name|Description|
|---|---|
|**Facilitation**|Guiding conversations and meetings to stay focused, inclusive, and productive.|
|**Assertive Communication**|Speaking with clarity and confidence, while being respectful and non-aggressive.|
|**Leadership Presence**|Creating a calm, focused, and confident energy that helps others trust and follow your lead.|
|**Situational Awareness**|Reading the room (even virtual), adjusting tone, timing, and pacing accordingly.|
|**Team Communication Management**|Making sure all voices are heard, blockers are addressed, and discussions stay timeboxed.|

---

### 🔑 Why This Matters:

This is **one of the most important daily skills** for any team lead, engineering manager, or aspiring tech architect. It shows:

- **Credibility** (you know what’s happening),
    
- **Empathy** (you care how people are feeling),
    
- **Influence** (you can steer conversations constructively),
    
- and **Decisiveness** (you know when to move forward).

### 🎙️ Sample Scripts for Different Situations

#### 🟢 Daily Standup Facilitation (Situational Scripts)

**1. Standard Daily Standup**

> "Morning, team! Let’s go around quickly—what you did yesterday, today’s plan, and any blockers. We’ll handle details after. [Name], you go first."

**2. Monday Standup – Kickstart the Week**

> "Happy Monday! Let’s align on goals for the week. Please share what you last worked on, today’s plan, and anything we should plan ahead for. [Name], please begin."

**3. Friday Wrap-Up Standup**

> "Good morning! Let’s give a quick wrap-up—share if everything’s on track or if anything is pending before the sprint ends. Any blockers to carry over? [Name], over to you."

**4. When a Sprint is Ending Soon**

> "Let’s be outcome-focused—share what’s remaining and any risks to completion. We'll tackle issues right after the round. [Name], kick us off."

**5. When Team is Going Off-Track During Standup**

> "Let’s stay on track—keep it short: yesterday, today, blockers. Deep dives can happen right after this. [Name], your turn."

**6. If Energy Is Low or People Are Tired**

> "Let’s keep it light and quick. Just your main update and any blockers. We’ll keep follow-ups short today. [Name], go ahead."

**7. If Everyone Is Quiet or Hesitant**

> "No worries if updates are short—just let us know if you’re stuck or need help. It’s okay to say 'no change'. [Name], want to start?"

**8. When Someone Is Blocked**

> "Thanks for calling that out. We’ll sync right after to resolve it—others, continue with your updates. [Next name], go ahead."

**9. Remote/Distributed Team**

> "Hi everyone! Let’s go async-friendly. Share your three updates—yesterday, today, blockers—and we’ll capture actions separately. [Name], please begin."

**10. New Team Member Joining**

> "Welcome [New Member]! Everyone, share your update quickly, and let’s help [Name] feel onboarded with what’s happening. [You], start us off."

**11. Too Many Details Being Shared**

> "Let’s move details to the post-call discussion. Just high-level updates now, please. [Name], go ahead."

**12. Mid-Sprint Alignment Standup**

> "Let’s pause for a quick check: Are we on track with our sprint goals? Same format—yesterday, today, blockers—with a focus on deliverables. [Name], over to you."

**13. After a Holiday or Long Break**

> "Hope you all had a good break! Let’s sync back—what’s your priority today and any blockers to ramp back up? [Name], please start."

**14. Before/After a Major Release**

> "We’re in release mode—share if anything’s at risk, or needs validation. Be concise. [Name], you’re up."

### 🟠 Sprint Planning Kick-off

> "Let’s make sure all stories have a clear goal and acceptance criteria. Please raise your hand if anything feels vague or overestimated. Remember to speak up early—we're planning for success here."

### 🔵 Sprint Review Opening

> "Great work reaching the end of this sprint! Today, we’ll walk through completed stories, demo features, and gather any feedback. [Name], can you start with the first item?"

### 🟡 Retrospective Start

> "This is our space to reflect—no judgments, only improvements. Let’s talk about what went well, what could have gone better, and what actions we’ll take. Feel free to drop your thoughts anonymously if needed."

### 🟣 One-on-One Opener

> "Hey [Name], how have things been lately—anything you’re enjoying or struggling with? Let’s also look at your goals and see if there’s any way I can support you better."

### ⚪ If Someone is Going Off Track in Standup

> "Let’s continue this detailed discussion after the standup so we don’t hold up others. I’ll stay back with you."

### 🔴 When Someone’s Stuck

> "Thanks for highlighting that blocker. Let’s quickly assign someone or take it up after the call. We’ll make sure it’s unblocked today."
