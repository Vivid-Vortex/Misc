**Question:**

> I’m a software engineer and recently became a team lead. Earlier, I used to only share my own updates during standups. Now, as a lead, how can I effectively guide my team during standups, scrum calls, and team meetings?

---

**Answer:**

The transition from individual contributor to a team lead is more about **servant leadership, communication, and orchestration** than just giving your own status. Here's how you can guide your team effectively in **daily standups, scrum calls, and other meetings**:

---

### 🟢 1. **Daily Standups**

The purpose of standup is **team alignment**. As a lead:

#### Your Role:

- Be the **facilitator**, not the only speaker.
    
- Set a **positive and focused tone** (timebox to ~15 mins).
    
- **Nudge clarity** if someone is vague (e.g., “I worked on some tickets” → “Can you briefly say which ones and what's next?”).
    
- Ensure blockers are **called out** and **actioned**.
    
- Track **patterns**: frequent blockers, delays, unclear tasks.
    

#### Format to Drive:

Go team member by member:

- What did you do yesterday?
    
- What will you do today?
    
- Any blockers?
    

> ✅ Tip: If the team is bigger than 6-7, group by modules or functional areas.

---

### 🟠 2. **Scrum Ceremonies**

#### Sprint Planning:

- Guide team to **break work into stories/tasks**.
    
- Ensure stories have clear **acceptance criteria**.
    
- Balance **capacity** (availability, leaves, past velocity).
    
- Ask **questions to challenge ambiguity**.
    

#### Sprint Review:

- Encourage the team to **demo working features**.
    
- Highlight key achievements and **give recognition**.
    
- Ask stakeholders for **feedback** (if external).
    

#### Retrospective:

- Make it safe for everyone to share what went well and what didn’t.
    
- Focus on **action items**, not blame.
    
- Keep a **running log** of improvements and follow-ups.
    

---

### 🟡 3. **Team Meetings (1:1s, Weekly Syncs, Ad-hoc discussions)**

#### In Team Meetings:

- Share **vision**, priorities, risks.
    
- Let everyone speak—rotate ownership of updates.
    
- Bring visibility into **dependencies** or cross-team blockers.
    

#### In 1:1s:

- Listen more than you speak.
    
- Discuss **growth, blockers, motivations**, not just tasks.
    
- Coach gently: “What’s your plan to resolve this?” instead of offering ready answers every time.
    

---

### 🔵 4. **As a Leader, Always Aim To…**

- **Connect the dots**: Link daily work to product goals.
    
- **Protect the team**: From scope creep, meetings overload, or unclear requests.
    
- **Keep track**: Use tools like Jira boards or dashboards to stay informed and guide better.
    
- **Be available**: Let the team know you’re approachable.
    

---

> “Good morning team! Let’s keep this crisp. Please share what you did yesterday, today’s plan, and any blockers. If anything needs deep dive, we’ll park it after the standup.”

## 🎙️ Sample Scripts for Different Situations

---

### 🟢 **Daily Standup Facilitation (Short & Efficient)**

> "Good morning, team! Let’s keep this crisp and stick to our three questions. If something needs deeper discussion, we’ll park it for after the standup. [Name], let’s start with you.”

---

### 🟠 **Sprint Planning Kick-off**

> “Let’s make sure all stories have a clear goal and acceptance criteria. Please raise your hand if anything feels vague or overestimated. Remember to speak up early—we're planning for success here.”

---

### 🔵 **Sprint Review Opening**

> “Great work reaching the end of this sprint! Today, we’ll walk through completed stories, demo features, and gather any feedback. [Name], can you start with the first item?”

---

### 🟡 **Retrospective Start**

> “This is our space to reflect—no judgments, only improvements. Let’s talk about what went well, what could have gone better, and what actions we’ll take. Feel free to drop your thoughts in the board/chat anonymously if needed.”

---

### 🟣 **One-on-One Opener**

> “Hey [Name], how have things been lately—anything you’re enjoying or struggling with? Let’s also look at your goals and see if there’s any way I can support you better.”

---

### ⚪ **If Someone is Going Off Track in Standup**

> “Let’s continue this detailed discussion after the standup so we don’t hold up others. I’ll stay back with you.”

---

### 🔴 **When Someone’s Stuck**

> “Thanks for highlighting that blocker. Let’s quickly assign someone or take it up after the call. We’ll make sure it’s unblocked today.”
